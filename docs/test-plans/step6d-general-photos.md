## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).
- Use an inspection with a job number, ideally with some item photos already.

## Steps
1. **Where.** On the inspection home, the Photos section has a **General photos** area with Take photo and Choose photos.
2. **Take and choose.** Take a general photo with the camera, and choose two from the library: all three show as thumbnails under "General photos (3)".
3. **Viewer.** Tap one: the viewer says "General · photo 1 of 3"; swiping, captions and Save work as for item photos.
4. **Delete asks.** Delete a general photo: it asks "Delete this photo?" first. Cancel keeps it; Delete removes it.
5. **Save general.** Tap **Save to iPad** under General photos and save to Files: names are like "SY000001 General 1.jpg". The camera shot is full size.
6. **Save everything.** Tap **Save photos to iPad** (whole inspection): item photos and general photos are all offered, general ones last.
7. **Free up space.** Works for saved general camera shots too.
8. **Kept.** Force-quit and reopen: general photos and their captions are still there.
9. **Delete the inspection** (a test one): its general photos go too, and storage drops.
10. **Offline.** Airplane mode: adding and viewing general photos works. Turn airplane mode off.
