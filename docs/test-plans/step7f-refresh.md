## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).

## Home
1. **Two columns.** In landscape: Recent inspections on the left, Projects on the right. In portrait, Projects sits below.
2. **Inspection cards.** Each is headed by what was inspected, with its job, client and date underneath, and a status chip: green "Memo SIM-…", amber "N items, no memo", red "Needs a project" or grey "No items yet".
3. **Projects.** Projects only (no inspections), each with a coloured badge of its initials. Search narrows them. Tap one: its project page opens (cards: details, inspections, contacts).
4. **Contacts boxes.** On the project page, the contact boxes are the same size as the other fields. So are the memo's recipient boxes.

## Inspection steps
5. **Step bar.** Open an inspection: under its title, a large bar with 1 Pre-inspection, 2 Inspection, 3 Site memo. The current one is maroon; each shows a status line (e.g. "Job details done", "1 drawing · 3 items", "SIM-001"); finished ones show a green tick.
6. **Stays at the top.** On Pre-inspection and Site memo, scroll down: the step bar stays in view.
7. **Straight into the drawings.** Tap **Inspection**: the drawings open directly (no list to tap through). The compact bar at the top shows the job and the three steps.
8. **No drawings yet.** On an inspection with no drawings, Inspection shows **Add drawings** instead.
9. **Drawings panel.** Tap **Drawings** in the toolbar: a panel lists the drawings with rename (pencil) and delete (bin). Tap a drawing: the view scrolls to it. Add drawings from here too.
10. **Upload several.** Add two PDFs at once (or one that isn't a PDF): the list stays up with its progress and any error until it's done.
11. **Toolbar.** Undo, Redo, Fit page (corners icon), camera and Add pin are there. In portrait it's all one row (Drawings and Items as icons) and the drawing keeps its height when the item editor opens.
12. **Camera.** Tap the camera, take a photo: "Photo added to General photos" shows briefly, and the drawing stays where it was.
13. **Photos on Site memo.** Open Site memo, scroll below the memo: Photos shows each item's photos under "Instruction A – …", then observations, then General photos, with Save photos to iPad and Free up space. Deleting a photo here asks first.

## Settings
14. **Layout.** Landscape: a section list on the left (My details, Signature, Prefilled messages, Storage and backup) beside cards with icons. Tapping a section jumps to it. Portrait: the sections show across the top.
15. **Folded messages.** Each prefilled message is one row (name and the start of its text). Tap to open and edit; tap again to fold. Chips at the top jump to memo messages, standard conditions and the notes box heading. **Add** opens the new one ready to type.
16. **Storage.** A bar shows space used; green "Kept by the iPad" or amber "May be cleared if space runs low".

## Dark mode
17. **Follows the iPad.** Switch the iPad to Dark (Control Centre or Settings → Display & Brightness): the app turns dark straight away; switch back and it's light again.
18. **Readable.** In dark mode check the home, an inspection's three steps, the item editor, the project page and Settings: all text and buttons are readable.
19. **Paper stays white.** In dark mode, drawing pages, the notes box, the memo preview and the signature box stay white.
20. **Offline.** In airplane mode everything above still works, including the icons.
