## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).
- Your existing inspections are moved into projects on first start: ones with the same job number share one project.

## Existing inspections
1. **Moved over.** Inspections: **Recent** lists your inspections (up to 10, newest first). Each still shows its job number and name.
2. **Projects tab.** Tap **Projects**: one project per job number, with its client and how many inspections. Any inspection that had no job number is listed first under **Needs a project**, with a red flag.
3. **Nothing lost.** Open a moved inspection: drawings, pins, photos and its memo are all there; Pre-inspection shows the project's details and the inspection's own (item inspected, date, inspector).

## Needs a project
4. **Opens on Pre-inspection.** Tap a flagged inspection: it opens on Pre-inspection with the **Needs a project** box.
5. **Create new project.** Tap it, enter a job number and name, **Create project**: the flag goes, and the client and address it had are filled in.
6. **Assign to project.** On another flagged one (or Skip for now in step 9), tap **Assign to project** and pick a project: it takes that project's details.
7. **No memo without a project.** On a flagged inspection, the Site memo tab says to put it in a project first.

## New inspections
8. **Choose a project.** Tap **New inspection**: pick a project from the list (try searching by job number, name or client): it opens on Pre-inspection with that project's details filled in.
9. **New project or skip.** New inspection again: type a new job number and name, **Create project**. Then once more with **Skip for now**: it's flagged Needs a project.
10. **Duplicate job number.** New inspection, type a job number that's already used (any case): it says the project exists and offers **Use that project**.

## Shared details
11. **Shared.** Two inspections in one project: change the client company on one's Pre-inspection, then open the other: it has changed there too, and in both memos.
12. **Change project.** On Pre-inspection, **Change project** to another: the details switch; its memo keeps its SIM number.

## Project page
13. **Open it.** Tap the project name on Pre-inspection (or the project in Projects): its details, inspections and contacts. **‹ Projects** goes back.
14. **New inspection here.** Tap **New inspection** on the project page: it starts in that project.
15. **Edit details.** Change the job name here: the inspections show the new name.
16. **Delete project.** Tap **Delete project**: it asks first and says how many inspections go with it. Cancel keeps everything; Delete removes the project and its inspections.

## Contacts
17. **Typed recipients saved.** In a memo, add a recipient (company and attn) and tap elsewhere: the project page's Contacts lists them (once, not twice), along with the client.
18. **Add from contacts.** In another inspection's memo in that project, **Add from contacts** lists them; picking one adds it (as Copy if there's already a To).
19. **Edit contacts.** On the project page, edit or remove a contact: the next memo's list follows.
20. **Offline.** In airplane mode, create a project and an inspection, and add a contact: all works.
