## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).
- Use an inspection with a drawing of two or more pages and a few pins.

## Copying
1. **Copy pin.** Open a pin, type its text, tap **Copy pin**: a dark hint at the top right says "Tap each spot for a copy of Instruction A" with **Done**; the sheet shows **Done copying**.
2. **Several taps.** Tap three spots on the drawing: each gets a pin with the same letter. Scroll to another page and tap there too: that page gets a copy and a notes box listing the item.
3. **Done.** Tap **Done** (hint or sheet): taps go back to normal.
4. **Same comment.** Change the text, or switch to Observation: every copy changes (colour and notes boxes).
5. **Letters don't move.** Put a copy of a later letter (say C) on page 1: A and B keep their letters; page 1's notes box lists A, B, then C.
6. **Tap a copy.** Tap a copy: the same item's sheet opens.
7. **Drag a copy.** Drag a copy: only that one moves.
8. **Arrows.** With the sheet open, **Add arrow**, then tap near one of the copies: the arrow comes from that copy.

## Removing
9. **The pins list.** The sheet lists the pins ("Page 1 (original)", "Page 2"…). Tap one: the drawing goes to it.
10. **Remove a copy.** Tap **Remove** beside a copy: just that pin goes. **Undo** brings it back.
11. **Remove the original.** Tap **Remove** beside "(original)": the first copy becomes the original (its letter may change if it's on a later page).
12. **Delete.** **Delete instruction A** removes every pin of it. Undo brings them all back.

## Elsewhere
13. **Items panel.** The item is listed once, with "3 pins" under its text.
14. **Pages view.** A page with only a copy shows the pin count and can't be hidden.
15. **Memo and PDF.** The memo lists the item once. Export: every copy is burned into its drawing page, and its photos appear once in the appendix.
