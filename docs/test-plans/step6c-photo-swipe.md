## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).
- Use an item with four or more photos.

## Steps
1. **Drag slowly.** Drag a photo sideways: it follows your finger smoothly, and you can see the next photo coming in beside it.
2. **Let go past a quarter.** It glides the rest of the way to the next photo, with no blank moment.
3. **Let go early.** Drag a little and let go slowly: it springs back to the same photo.
4. **Quick flick.** A short, fast flick changes photo, like the Photos app.
5. **Fast in a row.** Flick several times quickly: each flick moves one photo, with no stutter or skipped photos.
6. **Ends.** At the first and last photo, dragging further resists and springs back.
7. **Caption kept.** Type a caption, swipe away and back: it's still there.
8. **Big photos.** With large photos, swiping is still smooth.
