## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).
- Open an inspection with a drawing, on the Inspection step, with no item or panel open. Try in landscape first, then portrait.

## Steps
1. **Controls on the right.** The Page / Items pill and the tools pill (Add pin, camera, undo, redo, fit) are stacked in the middle of the right edge, clear of the notes box at the top right of a page.
2. **Panels on the left (landscape).** Tap **Items**: the list opens on the left and the drawing moves to the right. Same for the page button (Drawings) and for an item's editor. In portrait they open at the bottom as before.
3. **Camera toast.** Take a general photo with the camera button: the "added" message appears to the left of the pill, fully on screen.
4. **Tap.** Tap an empty spot: the red pin appears at once; its editor opens a moment later (about a third of a second).
5. **Double-tap, ten times.** Double-tap ten different empty spots at a normal double-tap speed: each gives one blue pin. Note how many of the ten worked first time.
6. **Double-tap a pin.** Double-tap an existing pin: it switches kind (red to blue, or blue to red).
7. **Double-tap and hold.** Tap, then press again in the same spot and hold: a blue ring fills, then drag: a blue arrow follows your finger. Lift: one blue observation pin with the arrow, editor open. Undo once removes both.
8. **Double-tap and hold, no drag.** Tap, press again and hold, lift without moving: one blue pin, no arrow.
9. **Hold still works.** Press and hold (no tap first): red ring, red pin, drag a red arrow, as before.
10. **Tap during the pause.** Tap a spot, then quickly tap somewhere far away (more than a finger-width): the second tap doesn't add a pin (the first pin counts as open); tap again to place one.
11. **Zoom.** Pinch in and out on a busy page several times. Is it still choppy while your fingers move, after you lift, or both? (Reply with which.)
12. **Scrolling.** Swipe quickly up and down: smooth, no ring or pin appears.
