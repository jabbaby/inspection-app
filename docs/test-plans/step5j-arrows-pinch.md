## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).
- Use an inspection with a few pins on a page.

## Pinch
1. **Pinch in and out** several times, including straight after scrolling. It should feel smoother than the last version, with no stalls at the start of a pinch.
2. **Pinch right after zooming.** Zoom in, let go, and pinch again straight away while the page is still sharpening: it stays smooth (the sharpening waits until you let go).

## Arrows
3. **Add an arrow.** Open an item, tap **Add arrow**: the sheet says "Tap the drawing where the arrow should point." Tap a spot on the same page: a line runs from the pin to that spot, with an arrowhead on the spot, in the pin's colour.
4. **Several arrows.** Add a second arrow from the same pin: the sheet says "Arrows (2)".
5. **Other page.** Tap Add arrow, then tap a different page: it doesn't add one and asks you to tap the pin's page. **Cancel** stops waiting.
6. **Move a tip.** With the item open, each tip has a hollow ring. Drag one: the arrow follows your finger and stays where you let go. The drawing doesn't scroll while you drag.
7. **Remove.** Tap a tip's ring (it fills in), then **Remove arrow**: that arrow goes. With only one arrow, Remove arrow works without selecting.
8. **Undo / Redo.** Undo and Redo step through adding, moving and removing arrows.
9. **Closed item.** Close the sheet: the arrows stay on the drawing, without the rings.
10. **Zoom.** Zoom in and out: arrows scale with the drawing and stay on their spots; pins stay the same size.
11. **Move the pin.** Drag a pin with arrows: the arrows stretch from the pin's new spot to the same tips.
12. **Observation arrows.** Switch an item with arrows to Observation: its arrows turn blue.
13. **Delete and undo.** Delete an item with arrows, then Undo: the arrows come back too.
14. **Force-quit and reopen.** The arrows are still there.
15. **Offline.** Airplane mode: adding and moving arrows works. Turn airplane mode off.

## Feel (free text)
Arrow thickness and arrowhead size on your real sheets; whether the tip rings are easy to grab.
