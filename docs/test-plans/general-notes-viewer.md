## General notes
1. **Add one.** Open Items and tap **Add general note**: an editor opens for "Observation A" saying it's a general note with no pin, with no Instruction / Observation switch, arrows or copy pin. Type a note and tap Done.
2. **Lettering.** With a pinned observation already there, the general note is A and the pinned one moves to B.
3. **Every box.** Every page now shows its notes box, each listing the general note first, then that page's own items.
4. **Items panel.** It's listed under Observations, "General (every notes box)". Tap it to edit; swipe to delete; Undo brings it back.
5. **Move a box.** On a page with no pins, drag its notes box somewhere else, leave and come back: it stays where you put it.
6. **Never an instruction.** In Items, Select the general note: Make instructions does nothing for it.
7. **Export.** Export the PDF pack: only pages with pins or markup are in it (no extra pages for the boxes), and each box lists the general note first.
8. **Delete the last.** Delete the general note: pages with no pins lose their boxes again.
9. **Backup.** Back up and import: the general note comes back as a general note.

## PDF viewer
10. **About.** Settings, About, User guide: the guide opens inside the app with a **Back** button at the top; scroll through it; Back returns to Settings.
11. **Preview.** Site memo, Export PDF, then **Preview**: the pack opens the same way, and Back returns to the Export card.
12. **Offline.** In Airplane Mode, open the user guide again: it still opens.

## Follow-up (2026-10-07): page sizes, zoom, Share
13. **Sizes.** Export a pack with an A1 (or A3) drawing and tap Preview: the memo page shows much narrower than the drawing page, each labelled underneath, e.g. "Page 1 · A4 (210 × 297 mm)" and "Page 2 · A1 (841 × 594 mm)".
14. **Zoom.** Pinch with two fingers on the pages, and use − and +: the pages zoom (not the whole app), and you can scroll around them.
15. **Share.** Tap **Share…** in the viewer's bar: the iPad Share sheet opens with the PDF. Save it to Files and open it there: its pages are the same sizes.
16. **Documents.** In Settings, About, open the User guide: Share… works there too.
