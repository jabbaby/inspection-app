## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).
- Use an inspection with **two or more drawings** (multi-page if possible), and Item inspected, Inspector and Date filled in.

## Steps
1. **Rename a drawing.** The dialog is wider and the full current name is visible.
2. **Open a drawing** from the inspection. All drawings appear as one scrolling document, with each drawing's name above its first page, opening at the drawing you tapped.
3. **Scroll with one finger** from the first drawing into the next. It is smooth; the label at the top changes (e.g. "S-102 Level 4 · page 1 of 3").
4. **Pinch to zoom** anywhere, including across the gap between two pages. Pages sharpen shortly after you stop.
5. **Scroll quickly to the end and back.** Pages appear as you reach them; the app doesn't reload or crash. Note any blank pages that stay blank.
6. **Fit page.** The page in the middle of the screen fits the screen.
7. **Add pins on different drawings and pages.** Letters run A, B, C… across everything, in the order you placed them.
8. **Observation pins** are filled blue; instruction pins are filled red.
9. **Notes box** (top right of each pinned page): blue border, header and observations, **red instructions**, all capitals, Arial. It shows the header, then **INSTRUCTIONS:** with that page's instructions, then **NOTED FOR INFORMATION:** with its observations. The header fits on one line for a typical item inspected.
10. **Switch an item** between Instruction and Observation. It moves between the two sections of the box and the pin changes colour.
11. **Delete a middle item** (e.g. B of A, B, C). C becomes B everywhere: pin, notes box and items lists. The next new pin takes the next letter in sequence.
12. **Delete a drawing** that has items. The remaining items are re-lettered with no gaps.
13. **Items button** in the toolbar. The list shows every item; tapping one scrolls to its pin (even on another drawing) and opens it; **‹ Items** goes back to the list; **Close** hides the panel.
14. **Portrait.** Open Items or an item: the panel sits below the document and the document doesn't jump.
15. **Force-quit and reopen.** Everything is still there.
16. **Offline.** Airplane mode, force-quit, reopen, open the document: scroll, add a pin, type, all fine. Turn airplane mode off.

## Feel (free text)
Scrolling and zooming across many pages; whether equal-width pages work for your real drawing sets; notes box size.
