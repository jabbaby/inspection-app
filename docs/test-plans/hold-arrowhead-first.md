Pins (Pin tool on)

1. **Hold, then drag.** Press on a spot and hold until the ring fills, then drag away. The arrowhead stays on the spot you pressed and the red pin follows your finger. Lift: the pin is where you lifted, its arrow (a dog leg) points back to the spot, and its editor opens.
2. **Undo.** Undo once: the pin and its arrow go together.
3. **Hold without dragging.** Press, hold until the ring fills, lift without moving: just a pin at that spot, no arrow.
4. **Edge of the page.** Hold near a page's edge and drag off the page or onto the next page: the pin stays on the page you pressed.
5. **Double-tap and hold.** Tap a spot, then press again straight away and hold: the pin turns blue. Drag: the arrowhead stays on the spot and the blue pin follows. Lift: an observation where you lifted, its arrow to the spot. One Undo removes it.
6. **Existing pin.** Wait a moment after placing a pin, then tap it, press it again straight away and hold, and drag: this pin stays where it is and its arrow follows your finger (as before). It becomes an observation.
7. **Pencil.** Repeat 1 and 5 with the Apple Pencil.

Text callouts (Text on)

8. **Hold, then drag.** Hold on a spot until the ring fills, then drag up and to the left. The arrowhead stays on the spot; a small box outline follows your finger, hanging up and left from it. Lift and type a long note: the box grows up and to the left, away from the arrow, and the leader comes out of the box's side facing the spot.
9. **Other directions.** Repeat dragging down-right, up-right and down-left: each time the box's corner nearest the arrowhead is where you lifted, and the box never covers the spot.
10. **Hold without dragging.** Hold and lift without moving: a box with no arrow, its top-left at the press (as with a tap).
11. **After placing.** Tap the new callout to select it, then drag its box, its arrow tip and its side handle: all work as before.
12. **PDF.** Export the PDF pack: the pins and callouts from steps 1 to 9 are where they were on screen.

Elsewhere

13. **Project badges.** On the Inspections page (Recent and Projects) and the Dashboard's Continue tile, each project's badge shows one letter: the first letter of its job name, skipping digits (e.g. "12 Smith St" shows S). A project whose name is only digits shows its job number's first letter.
14. **Settings warning.** Settings, Storage and backup starts with an amber note: "Don't remove Hardhat from the Home Screen…", in both light and dark mode, and in portrait.
