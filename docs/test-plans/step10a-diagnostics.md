The panel shows page numbers and paper sizes only, never drawing names, so screenshots of a real drawing are fine to send; crop the drawing itself out if you prefer.

The panel

1. **Off by default.** Open an inspection's drawings: no diagnostics panel.
2. **Switch on.** Settings, Drawing, turn on **Show diagnostics**. Back in the drawings, a charcoal **Diagnostics** panel sits at the top left, listing the zoom, each page on screen with its paper size, and Base and Sharp lines.
3. **Live.** Zoom in and out and scroll: within about a second the numbers change (zoom, pages on screen, Sharp lines). At fit width the Sharp lines say "not needed".
4. **Hide and Copy.** Tap **×**: it folds to a small Diagnostics chip; tap the chip to open it again. Tap **Copy**, then paste into Notes: the same lines, with the app version at the top.
5. **Off again.** Turn the switch off in Settings: the panel is gone from the drawings.

The three readings (use a real large drawing, ideally an A1 sheet)

6. **Blurry zoom.** Zoom into a busy part of a real A1 drawing until it looks blurry (or as far as you normally zoom), wait two seconds, and screenshot the screen with the panel. Say whether it looks sharp or blurry.
7. **Scrolling with markup.** On a page with a lot of markup, scroll steadily past it for two or three seconds, stop, and screenshot (the Scroll line needs a scroll first). Say how smooth it felt.
8. **Scrolling without markup.** Do the same on a page with little or no markup, and screenshot, to compare.

Elsewhere

9. **No effect while off.** With the switch off, drawing, pins, scrolling and zoom feel the same as before.
