## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).
- Try each section in landscape and portrait, and once in dark mode.

## The rail
1. **Rail.** A charcoal strip down the left: the red Northrop "N" roundel at the top, **Inspections** and **Settings** (icon over a small label; the current one is a light tile), and a small green dot at the bottom.
2. **Offline.** Airplane mode on: the dot becomes an amber **Offline** badge. Off again: the green dot returns.
3. **Screen header.** Inside an inspection, a light row at the top: a round back button, the job and item inspected, and the three steps as a pill switcher (ticks on finished steps), with Saving…/Saved on the right. Back still returns to where you came from (project or Inspections).

## Home
4. **Header.** The NORTHROP wordmark, today's date and "Good morning, {name}", with search and **New inspection** on the right.
5. **Continue tile.** A large tile with the last inspection: its project badge and name, a live preview of its busiest drawing page zoomed in around the pins (pins drawn on top, letters readable), the three-part progress bar, counts, and a dark pill button for the next step.
6. **Preview after rotating.** Rotate the iPad: the drawing preview redraws to fill its tile (no dark gap at the side).
7. **Number tiles.** **Items logged** this month on a warm cream-to-peach gradient (maroon number); **Needs attention** on a charcoal gradient (count plus up to three inspections to tap; "All clear" when none); **Memos this month** with the latest SIM reference; **Storage** as a ring.
8. **Needs attention links.** Tap an inspection in the Needs attention tile: it opens on the step that fixes it.
9. **Recent table.** Columns Inspection, Project (with the client underneath), Date, Status, grouped This week / Earlier. In portrait the Date column hides. Projects sits beside it (below in portrait) with **New project**.
10. **Search.** Typing hides the tiles and narrows Recent and Projects.

## Drawings
11. **Tile.** The drawing sits in a large rounded tile under the header.
12. **Tool pill.** A charcoal pill on the left edge: red **Add pin** circle at the top, then camera, Undo, Redo, Fit page. Tap Add pin: it turns white with a red ring and a red **Tap the drawing to place the pin** hint shows at the top; tap the drawing to place the pin.
13. **Chips.** The drawing and page chip is top right (opens Drawings); **Items** with its count is bottom right.
14. **Page label.** In portrait with two pages on screen, the page label names the page filling the top of the view (not the one below it).
15. **Item editor.** A rounded tile beside the drawing (below in portrait); **Done** is charcoal, Delete is quiet red.
16. **Feel.** Pins, pinch, scrolling and dragging pins/arrows/notes box feel exactly as before.

## Other screens
17. **Pre-inspection, memo, Settings, project page.** White rounded tiles with no outlines (thin outlines in dark mode), pill buttons and switches, titles in the same style as the home greeting.

## Dashboard, Inspections tab, swipe to delete, status bar (added later on 2026-10-04)
18. **Rail.** Three tabs: **Dashboard**, **Inspections**, **Settings**. The app opens on Dashboard.
19. **Dashboard.** Greeting, **New inspection** and the tiles (Continue, Items logged, Needs attention, Memos this month, Storage). No Recent or Projects here.
20. **Inspections tab.** Search, **New inspection**, the Recent table and Projects.
21. **Back buttons.** An inspection opened from the Dashboard goes **Back to dashboard**; from the Inspections tab, **Back to inspections**; from a project, **Back to project**.
22. **Swipe to delete.** On the Inspections tab, swipe a Recent row left: a red **Delete** appears (the row doesn't open). Tap the row: it closes. Swipe again and tap Delete: it asks first; Cancel keeps it, Delete removes it.
23. **Status bar.** On every screen (Dashboard, Inspections, Settings, inside an inspection, portrait and landscape), the clock, date and battery sit on a charcoal strip at the top, and nothing on the page goes under them.
