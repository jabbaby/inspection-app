## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).
- Use an inspection with **two or more drawings** (multi-page if possible), at least one instruction and one observation on the same page, and Item inspected, Inspector and Date filled in.

## Steps
1. **Flick up with one finger.** The document keeps rolling after you lift your finger and slows down smoothly to a stop, like GoodNotes.
2. **Flick harder.** It rolls further and faster; a gentle flick rolls only a little.
3. **Drag slowly, stop, then lift.** It doesn't roll after a finger held still.
4. **Scroll down with a slightly slanted finger.** The document moves straight up and down, with no sideways drift.
5. **Zoom in, then drag sideways.** It moves straight across. A clearly diagonal drag moves freely.
6. **Touch the screen while it is rolling.** It stops at once. Nothing else happens (no pin opens, no item sheet).
7. **Add pin, flick, then tap while it rolls.** The tap only stops the roll. Tap again: the pin is placed.
8. **Flick towards the end of the document.** It stops at the last page without bouncing past it.
9. **Tap an item in the Items list, or Fit page, while rolling.** The roll stops and the view jumps as usual.
10. **Pinch to zoom and lift both fingers.** No roll after a pinch.
11. **Notes box order.** Header first, then **NOTED FOR INFORMATION:** with the observations, then **INSTRUCTIONS:** with the instructions.
12. **Notes box colours.** Header line in black; border and observations blue; instructions red.
13. **Switch an item** between Instruction and Observation. It moves between the two sections, and the order stays observations then instructions.
14. **Offline.** Airplane mode, force-quit, reopen: flicking and the notes box work the same. Turn airplane mode off.

## Feel (free text)
Roll distance and how quickly it slows down compared with GoodNotes; whether the axis lock ever gets in the way when zoomed in.
