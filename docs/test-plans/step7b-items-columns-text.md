## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).
- Use an inspection with at least one observation and two instructions.

## Items on the inspection home
1. **Landscape.** Hold the iPad in landscape: Observations are on the left and Instructions on the right, side by side, both starting at the same height.
2. **Portrait.** Turn to portrait: Observations sit above Instructions, full width, as before.
3. **One kind only.** On an inspection with only instructions (or only observations), the one list uses the full width.
4. **Tapping still works.** Tap an item in either column: the drawing opens at its pin.

## Text boxes
5. **Prefilled messages.** In Settings, Prefilled messages: the message text is the same size and font as the Name box above it.
6. **Grows to fit.** Type a long message (several lines): the box grows so all the text shows, without scrolling inside it.
7. **Rotate.** Rotate the iPad with a long message showing: the box resizes to fit the new width.
8. **Memo editor.** In the memo editor, the message text and the instruction boxes (Conditions) are the same size as the other fields, and grow to fit their text.
9. **Still saves.** Edit a message in Settings and in the memo, leave the screen and come back: the edits are kept.

## Memo recipients table
10. **Top line.** In the memo preview, the recipients table (To | Copy | Company | Attn) has a grey line along the top of its cream heading row, like the line under it.
