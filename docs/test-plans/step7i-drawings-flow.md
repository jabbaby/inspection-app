## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).

## Steps
1. **Drawings on Pre-inspection.** Open an inspection's Pre-inspection: under This inspection there's a **Drawings** card. Add two PDFs from Files: they're listed with page counts; rename and delete work (delete asks first).
2. **Open at a drawing.** Tap the second drawing in that card: the Inspection step opens at it.
3. **Start the inspection.** **Start the inspection** opens the drawings.
4. **One document.** On the Inspection step, scroll from the first drawing into the second: no drawing names between them, just the next page.
5. **Page pill.** A charcoal pill in the middle of the right edge shows **Page N of M**, counting through every drawing (e.g. the second drawing's first page of two 3-page drawings is Page 4 of 6). Tap it: the Drawings panel opens.
6. **Clear of the notes box.** The right pill doesn't cover the notes box at the top right of a page.
7. **Items.** Under the page button, **Items** with a red count. Tap it: the list groups items by **Page 1**, **Page 4** and so on (no drawing names).
8. **Tap closes panels.** With Items open, tap an item (its editor opens), tap the drawing (the editor closes, Items stays), tap the drawing again (Items closes). Same for the Drawings panel.
9. **Tab memory.** In an inspection's Site memo, tap **Settings** in the rail, then **Inspections**: you're back on the same Site memo. Tap **Inspections** again: the list.
10. **Page memory.** Scroll the drawings to page 3, go to Dashboard, then tap Inspections: the drawings reopen at page 3.
