## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).
- Use an inspection with a job number and a few pins. Allow camera access when asked.

## Adding photos
1. **Take photo.** Open an item, tap **Take photo**: the camera opens. Take a photo and confirm: a thumbnail appears under "Photos (1)".
2. **Portrait and landscape.** Take one holding the iPad upright and one on its side: both thumbnails and full-screen views are the right way up.
3. **Choose photos.** Tap **Choose photos**, pick three from the library at once: all three appear, in order.
4. **Camera app.** Take a photo with the iPad's Camera app, then add it with Choose photos: it appears, and it's already in your Photos.
5. **Big photos.** Choose a few large ones (e.g. 48 MP or panoramas): they add without the app reloading or crashing.
6. **Counts.** The Items lists (inspection home and Items tab) show the item's photo count.

## Viewing
7. **Full screen.** Tap a thumbnail: it opens full screen. Use ‹ and › to move between photos.
8. **Caption.** Type a caption, close and reopen the photo: the caption is kept. A photo without a caption shows none.
9. **Delete and undo.** Delete a photo in the viewer, close it, then tap Undo in the toolbar: the photo comes back in the same place. Redo removes it again.
10. **With the item.** Delete an item that has photos, then Undo: its photos come back too.

## Saving to the iPad
11. **From the item.** Tap **Save to iPad** in the item sheet, then **Save N photos**: the Share sheet opens. Choose **Save Images**: the photos appear in Photos. Camera shots are full resolution (check the size in Photos' info panel).
12. **Save to Files.** Repeat with **Save to Files** into a folder: the files are named like "SY000001 Instruction A 1.jpg".
13. **Whole inspection.** On the inspection home, the Photos section shows how many photos there are and how much space the full-size copies take. Tap **Save photos to iPad** and save: every photo in the inspection is offered.
14. **Batches.** With more than 20 photos, the dialog saves them in batches ("Batch 1 of 2…"); each batch opens its own Share sheet.
15. **Cancel.** Close the Share sheet without saving: nothing is marked saved, and you can try again.
16. **Free up space.** After saving, tap **Free up space** and confirm: the full-size figure goes away and the photos still show in the app. Photos already in your Photos app are unaffected.

## Other
17. **Force-quit and reopen.** All photos, captions and counts are still there.
18. **Offline.** Airplane mode: taking, choosing, viewing and saving photos all work. Turn airplane mode off.
19. **Storage.** Settings shows storage used going up as you add photos.
