## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).
- Use an inspection with instructions on **two or more pages** (several on one page).

## Steps
1. **Page groups.** In the Items tab, under Observations and under Instructions, items sit under a subheading per page, e.g. "S-101 Level 3 · page 1".
2. **Row text.** Each row shows just the letter and text, with no "Instruction · drawing, page" line.
3. **Handles.** The ≡ handle only appears on pages with two or more items of that kind, and a drag only moves within that page group.
4. **Undo and Redo icons.** The toolbar shows a back arrow (Undo) and a forward arrow (Redo) instead of words, greyed out when there's nothing to do.
5. **Redo.** Delete an item, Undo it, then Redo: it's deleted again. Undo once more brings it back.
6. **Redo after a reorder.** Reorder on a page, Undo, then Redo: the new order returns.
7. **New action clears Redo.** Undo something, then make a new change (delete or reorder): Redo greys out.
8. **Done button.** Place a pin and type: a red **Done** button sits just above Delete. Tap it: the sheet closes and the text is saved.
9. **Pins right after scrolling.** Scroll, then quickly Add pin and tap: the pin lands exactly where you tapped (on the page you see).
10. **Offline.** Airplane mode: all of the above still works. Turn airplane mode off.
