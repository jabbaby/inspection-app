For readings, tap **Copy** and paste the text; don't send screenshots of real drawings. This replaces steps 1 to 5 of the step 10b plan (steps 6 to 9 there still apply).

First time through

1. **Storage before.** Settings, Storage and backup: note the "used" figure.
2. **Let it save.** Open the drawings and leave the iPad still on one page for about two minutes (screen on). Pages are drawn and saved in the background, nearest first; nothing on screen should move or flicker.
3. **Storage after.** Back in Settings: the used figure has grown, by roughly 1–3 MB per page of the set (say how much).

After saving

4. **Flick through.** Back in the drawings, flick steadily through the whole set and stop. Pages should appear sharp at once, with no soft preview. Copy the panel and paste it: page Base lines say "from saved image".
5. **Busy sheets.** Scroll to the busy sheets (10 to 12 last time): they appear at once. Copy a reading after stopping on them.
6. **Same look.** Compare a page at fit width with how it looked before: identical (no softening). Zoom in: it sharpens from the PDF as before.
7. **Next session.** Close the app completely (swipe it away), open it again and go to the drawings: pages still appear at once.
8. **Worst frame.** If any scroll still hitches, Copy straight after: the Scroll line now says what was busy during the worst frame ("during …").

Housekeeping

9. **Interrupted.** Start scrolling while the background saving is under way (soon after opening a set that isn't saved yet): scrolling isn't held up, and saving carries on once you stop.
10. **Rotate.** Rotate the iPad to portrait and wait: pages are drawn and saved again for portrait (a second set; storage grows again).
11. **Deleting.** On a test inspection (not real work), delete a drawing (or the inspection): its saved images go too. The used figure in Settings drops (it may take a reopen of the app).
