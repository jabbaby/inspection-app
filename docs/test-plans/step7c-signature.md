## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).
- Fill in My details (name and title) in Settings.
- Have a photo of your signature on white paper in Photos (or take one when asked).

## Settings
1. **Signature section.** Settings → My details shows a Signature box with "No signature yet.", **Draw signature** and **Upload image**.
2. **Pad opens.** Tap **Draw signature**: a dialog with a white signing pad and a line.
3. **Pencil feel.** Sign with Apple Pencil: the line follows the tip with no visible lag or gaps, even when signing fast. Pressing harder makes the line thicker.
4. **Finger.** Clear, then sign with a finger: it draws too, and the page doesn't scroll or zoom while you sign.
5. **Palm.** Clear, sign with the Pencil while resting your palm on the pad: the palm leaves no marks.
6. **Clear and Cancel.** **Clear** empties the pad. **Cancel** closes it and keeps the old signature.
7. **Save.** Sign and tap **Save**: the signature appears in the box, cropped close to the ink.
8. **Kept.** Close and reopen the app: the signature is still there.

## Memo
9. **New memo gets it.** Create a memo on an inspection: under Sent via, **Include signature** is ticked and your signature shows. The preview shows it between "Yours sincerely," and your name, about 14 mm tall.
10. **Leave it off.** Untick **Include signature**: it leaves the preview; the name moves back up. Tick it again: it returns.
11. **Upload a photo.** Tap **Upload image** and choose (or take) a photo of your signature on paper: the box shows just the ink, with no paper-coloured rectangle, and the preview updates. Check it looks clean on the memo.
12. **Use my saved signature.** Tap it: the memo goes back to the one from Settings.
13. **Remove.** Tap **Remove**: the memo has no signature; the preview shows none.
14. **Only this memo.** Draw a new signature in Settings: the memo you already made keeps its own. A memo created after that gets the new one.
15. **First one saves to Settings.** Remove the signature in Settings, then draw one on a memo: Settings now shows it too.
16. **Offline.** In airplane mode, draw and upload signatures and check the preview: all works.
