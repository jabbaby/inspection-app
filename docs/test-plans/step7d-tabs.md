## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).
- Use an inspection with drawings, pins, photos and a memo, and keep one new one for step 1.

## Tabs
1. **New inspection.** Tap **New inspection**: it opens on **Pre-inspection** (highlighted), showing the job details.
2. **Existing inspection.** Go back to Inspections and open an existing one: it opens on the **Inspection** tab.
3. **Pre-inspection.** Shows Job details and **Delete inspection**, nothing else. Edits still save as you type.
4. **Inspection.** Shows **Open markup** (red), then Drawings, Items and Photos. On an inspection with no drawings, it says to add one instead of Open markup.
5. **Site memo.** With no memo: **Create memo** (greyed out, with a link to Pre-inspection, if the job number or name is missing). With a memo: the editor, headed "Site Instruction Memo · SIM-…", with the preview.
6. **Create from the tab.** On an inspection without a memo, tap **Create memo**: the editor appears in the same tab.
7. **Back button.** Move between tabs, then use the browser/app Back gesture: it steps back through the tabs.

## Markup (viewer)
8. **Open markup.** Tap **Open markup**: the drawings open at the top.
9. **Tabs in the viewer.** The viewer's top bar shows Pre-inspection | Inspection | Site memo, with Inspection highlighted, then the drawing and page, then the buttons.
10. **Jump from the viewer.** Tap **Site memo** in the viewer: the memo opens. Tap **Inspection**: back to the drawings list.
11. **Portrait.** Hold the iPad in portrait: tabs and the page label on the first row, Undo/Redo/Items/Fit page/Add pin on the second, at the right.
12. **Toolbar stays put.** In portrait and landscape, tap **Add pin** (it becomes "Tap the drawing…"): nothing in the bar moves and the drawing doesn't shift. Place a pin: it lands exactly where you tapped.
13. **Long names.** With a long drawing name, the label ends in "…" rather than wrapping.
14. **Drawing and item links.** On the Inspection tab, tap a drawing name and then (going back) an item: each opens the viewer at that drawing or pin.
