## Before you start
- Open the home-screen app and tap **Update now** if offered (or close and reopen it).
- Use an inspection with **two or more drawings**, ideally one that already has pins on several pages.

## Steps
1. **Existing items after the update.** Instructions read A, B, C… in order through the document (first drawing's pages first), and observations the same, separately.
2. **Pin on a later page first.** On an empty inspection, put a pin on page 2: it's instruction A. Go back to page 1 and add a pin: it becomes A, and page 2's pin becomes B (pin, notes box and lists all update).
3. **Several pins on one page.** They're lettered in the order you placed them, not by where they are on the page.
4. **Switch kind / delete.** Switching or deleting re-letters that kind in document order, with no gaps.
5. **Second drawing.** A pin on the second drawing comes after every pin on the first drawing.
6. **Inspection home, Items.** An **Observations** heading above the observations, then an **Instructions** heading above the instructions. Empty groups have no heading.
7. **Items panel in the drawings view.** Same two headings.
8. **Notes box position.** On a page with no pins yet, add a pin: the new box sits about 15 mm in from the top and right edges of the sheet, roughly in line with the title block's right edge, on A1 and on A3/A4 sheets. Boxes you placed before keep their spot.
9. **Offline.** Airplane mode, force-quit, reopen: all of the above still works. Turn airplane mode off.
